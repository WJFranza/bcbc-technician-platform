Main product

BCBC Technician Tools & USB Learning System

A guided bootable environment for learning Linux, understanding systems, checking hardware, recovering files, and building practical confidence.

Request early accessView roadmap

What makes it different

  • Technician-guided, not random-video-guided.
  • Built around safe exploration and clear recovery workflows.
  • Designed for beginners without insulting their intelligence.
  • Expandable into classes, kits, AI assistance, and business training.

Version 1 experience

  • Boot the USB
  • Choose a guided path: learn, inspect, recover, document
  • Run simple diagnostics and collect readable results
  • Learn the why behind each step

Future modules

  • AI clip and image education/tester
  • Local AI troubleshooting guide
  • Network and Wi-Fi fundamentals
  • Privacy-at-home lessons
  • Mesh/security edge-node concepts
